# Product scope

## Completed in the prototype

- Public landing page, pricing preview, support/safety links, and signup/login screens
- Eight-step onboarding flow with saved progress, age check, terms acceptance, demo OTP, verification status, profile preview, and preferences
- Fictional professional profiles, shift fit, privacy-aware profile detail, discovery filters, likes, passes, matches, and refresh batches
- Chat list and threads with local text persistence, mock photo sharing, read states, match expiry, reporting, and blocking
- Member settings, notification preferences, quiet hours, privacy controls, plan selection, report history, and deletion request submission
- Separate staff demo area for verification, reports, member management, deletion requests, subscriptions, and content drafts
- Responsive PWA shell, offline app-shell cache, keyboard focus, dialog roles, toast announcements, and reduced-motion handling

## Mocked features

- Account signup/login and staff access
- SMS verification and NPI/manual credential checks
- Profile/photo/document uploads
- Match recommendations and reciprocal likes
- Pre-match credits, plans, refunds, and subscriptions
- Message delivery, read receipts, mock photo sharing, typing state, and conversation expiration
- Reports, blocking, suspensions, warnings, content removal, and deletion request handling
- Notification delivery and micro-date invitations
- Admin metrics and activity summaries

## Deferred production features

- Secure identity and session management, account recovery, and role-based authorization
- Backend APIs, database, data encryption, audit history, retention, and deletion processing
- Real professional credential verification and appeals
- Real payments, refunds, subscriptions, and tax handling
- Real-time messaging, media upload/storage, video calling, and push notifications
- Location-aware discovery and schedule-based recommendations
- Operational moderation tooling, support workflows, incident response, and analytics
- Legal review, privacy impact assessment, accessibility audit, and security testing

## Recommended milestones

1. Define product requirements, eligibility rules, safety operations, privacy boundaries, and measurable launch criteria.
2. Design a secure backend and authentication/authorization model; define data lifecycle and audit requirements.
3. Implement and test onboarding, verification, profiles, preferences, discovery, and mutual matching behind service interfaces.
4. Implement consent-based chat, moderation, blocking/reporting, account deletion, and staff workflows.
5. Add payment and notification providers only after legal, reliability, and privacy review.
6. Run accessibility, privacy, security, performance, and cross-device QA before a controlled beta.
